# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro (static output), deployed to GitHub Pages from `sn-Victor.github.io` by a GitHub Actions workflow. The user chose "a framework (Astro/Next)"; Astro was picked because the site is static content with a few interactive islands (video players), and Astro ships zero JS by default.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring managers screening Victor Curran for software engineering internships and roles. They arrive from a résumé, LinkedIn, or GitHub, spend a minute or two, and decide whether to reach out or open a case study.

## Product Purpose

Victor's portfolio. Success is a recruiter understanding, quickly, that Victor designs, builds, ships and runs real production systems end to end, then emailing him or opening a case study.

## Positioning

The work is real and in production at a real business (Settlemyre Nursery, a family-owned garden center), not class projects: a shared texting inbox used by 9 staff, an accounting pipeline posting hundreds of bills a month to QuickBooks, and a headless retail + wholesale storefront that passes Core Web Vitals. Each has a written case study and a 22-second demo video.

## Operating Context

- Visitors skim on laptops and phones between other candidates.
- Case studies live in public GitHub repos; the portfolio links out to them.
- Demo videos (MP4, 1920×1080, 22 s, with music) and posters exist for all three case studies.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site on GitHub Pages; no server, no forms backend.
- Contact by email (curranvs@appstate.edu), LinkedIn, GitHub. **No phone number** on the public page (user decision).
- Education: B.S. Computer Science + Data Science Certificate, Appalachian State University, graduating **Dec 2027** (user confirmed); earlier coursework at Southern New Hampshire University (Jan 2022 – Dec 2024).
- Older content stays as a secondary section: Digital Labs 365 (co-founder), Jade-Trade, PrimescapeNC.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Victor Curran. Handle: sn-Victor.
- The portfolio is Victor's, as a CS student growing a developer portfolio. It must not be themed around, branded by, or tied to Settlemyre Nursery (user, 2026-11-12): no nursery/garden visual world, no Settlemyre logo or colors as identity. Settlemyre appears only as a factual employer/client name inside the work.
- Voice: plain, specific, first person; claims backed by the case studies (no generic buzzwords).

## Product Principles

1. Show the work first: the case studies and their videos are the headline.
2. Every claim traces to a case study or the user's own prior copy.
3. A recruiter gets the point in one screen and can reach Victor in one click.
4. Fast and light: the page itself must feel like the Core Web Vitals claim it makes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Videos are muted-by-default previews with visible controls and captions-free on-screen text; respect prefers-reduced-motion (no autoplay motion for those users). WCAG AA contrast.
